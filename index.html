📋 Before You Begin

How This Game Works

Read carefully — the Start button unlocks after 1 minute and 30 seconds!

👾
Choose a monster from the grid. Each one has 10 math questions. Answer them all correctly to slay it!
⚡
Pick your answer, then tap ✅ That's My Answer! to lock it in. You can change your mind before you tap that button.
📖
Every question has a 15-second reading timer. The answer choices stay locked until time is up — read carefully!
💥
Wrong answers let the monster zap you back! Lose too many HP and you'll be defeated. You can retry — but retry answers are only worth 5 points instead of 10.
🔊
Every question has a 🔊 Read Aloud button. Tap it to hear the question read out loud with each word highlighted as it's spoken.
💡
If you missed a question on a retry, a 💡 Show Hint button appears to help you out.
🏆
Defeat all 5 monsters to top the leaderboard! Your score is saved — the top scores win bonus points!
